01 / Context

Bringing nature back into the rhythm of home.

As cities become denser and everyday routines increasingly disconnect from nature, OASIS explores how indoor gardening can restore moments of calm, care, and wellbeing. The project transforms cultivation from a decorative afterthought into an intuitive daily ritual.

Brief

A living presence, not a decorative object.

Design a contemporary indoor gardening system that transforms plant care into a meaningful daily ritual. Through modularity, natural materials, and intuitive interaction, OASIS reconnects people with nature within compact urban homes.

02 / Research

Less space. More living.

The research followed adaptable interiors, indoor gardening, compact urban living, and biophilic design to understand how greenery can become part of domestic architecture without demanding a large footprint.

Adaptable living systems

Contemporary homes serve multiple functions throughout the day. Flexible, modular objects can respond to changing routines while maintaining visual calm.

Indoor gardening & wellbeing

Growing plants indoors can improve wellbeing, reduce stress, and strengthen the relationship between people and nature through recurring acts of care.

Compact urban living

Space-efficient products must support storage, planting, and daily activity without compromising clarity or ease of use.

Biophilic interior design

Natural materials, living greenery, and soft organic forms create restorative interiors and strengthen the connection between users and their surroundings.

03 / User research & trend

Plant care becomes part of interior identity.

01

Plants as interior identity

Height, colour, species, pot design, and placement allow greenery to become a deliberate architectural presence within the home.

02

Curated rather than cluttered

A single coordinated structure organizes individual plants into a unified composition, giving a collection a clear visual hierarchy.

03

The ritual of plant care

Watering, observing growth, removing leaves, rotating plants, and responding to seasons create an ongoing relationship between user and object.

05 / Moodboard

A quiet landscape indoors.

Soft daylight, sculptural vessels, warm mineral surfaces, and living vegetation establish a visual language that feels grounded, calm, and architectural.

06 / About OASIS

One structure. A changing relationship with nature.

Three planting vessels are arranged vertically around a central column. Their staggered composition creates a changing relationship between form, colour, and vegetation while preserving a compact footprint.

How can caring for plants become a quiet ritual of everyday living?

07 / Concept & development

A compact vertical landscape.

The concept develops from modular vessels arranged around a central column. Different heights and orientations allow plants to coexist vertically rather than compete for floor space.

01

Conceptual exploration

Sketches examined how a simple vertical structure could organize greenery, support care, and become a calm sculptural presence in the home.

02

Form through repetition

Rotating vessels around the central column established a changing silhouette while keeping each planting level accessible.

03

3D modelling with Rhino

Digital development refined the hemispherical vessels, central column, modular connections, balance, and spatial rhythm across each configuration.

04

Blender rendering

Material, vegetation, colour, and lighting studies tested how the same geometry could adapt to different contemporary interiors.

05

Post-production as a design tool

Colour, contrast, shadow, and texture were refined to evaluate material character and communicate the relationship between vegetation and structure.

08 / Prototyping

Form tested through three physical stages.

Each iteration examined proportion, stability, plant accessibility, capacity, and the relationship between levels before progressing toward the final digital system.

01

Volume & relationship

An early clay study explored how the central column and offset bowls could form one composition.

02

Scale & balance

A lightweight full-scale mock-up translated the proportions into context and tested height, spacing, and visual balance.

03

Use & accessibility

The final prototype refined the geometry and used real vegetation to test planting access and the relationship between plant levels.

09 / CMF exploration

One system, five interior characters.

Colour allows the same modular structure to either blend with vegetation, recall traditional ceramics, or become a restrained architectural object.

01 / Botanical Ground

Olive Green

A muted finish that visually connects the structure to the foliage it supports.

02 / Warm Earth

Terracotta

A tactile, familiar colour drawn from traditional ceramic planters and Mediterranean material culture.

03 / Quiet Canvas

Soft White

A light architectural presence that allows plants and flowers to become the primary focus.

04 / Sculptural Shadow

A strong silhouette and neutral background for colourful vegetation.

05 / Natural Calm

Soft Beige

A warm neutrality that integrates with timber, plaster, stone, and tactile domestic materials.

10 / Technical details

Modular dimensions and configurations.

A 250 mm bowl radius gives plants generous volume while maintaining a compact footprint. The system grows from a 525 mm single planter to 1000 mm and 1400 mm vertical arrangements.

11 / Assembly

Built to grow, one module at a time.

Repeatable structural sections connect vertically above the weighted planter base. Each bowl is added in sequence, allowing the object to expand as plants, routines, and environments change.

12 / A living system

Different scales. Different ways of living.

Users can begin with a compact single planter and gradually expand the system as their interior, collection, or relationship with plants evolves.

Designed to grow with you

A living structure for a more connected everyday.

Through planting, watering, observing, and rearranging, OASIS brings nature closer to daily life—not as decoration, but as something we nurture, experience, and grow alongside.
